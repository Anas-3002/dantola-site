Surgical · guided placement

Dental implants

A titanium or zirconia post becomes the root your crown screws into, so the tooth next to it stays untouched. We plan every implant on a 3D CBCT scan and place it through a printed surgical guide, which is why our patients report less swelling and faster healing.

Stage 1

CBCT scan, plan and guided placement of the implant.

Stage 2

Healing of 8–12 weeks while the bone integrates.

Stage 3

Scan, then the final zirconia or ceramic crown is fitted.

Honest caveat: implants require adequate bone and healthy gums. If grafting or a sinus lift is needed first, we will tell you at the planning stage — not after you have paid a deposit.

Good cosmetic dentistry is conservative. We start by designing the result digitally on your own photographs, trial it in your mouth in composite, and only then decide whether enamel needs to be touched at all.

Endodontics

Root canal therapy

When the nerve of a tooth is infected, a root canal saves the tooth instead of removing it. Rotary files and an electronic apex locator let us clean and seal the canal system in a single visit for most straightforward cases.

How it feels

The tooth is fully anaesthetised first. Most patients describe pressure rather than pain, and soreness for one to two days afterwards is normal.

Why it matters

A root-treated tooth continues to function normally. Leaving an infected tooth untreated risks abscess, bone loss and swelling.

We finish with a protective crown or onlay where needed, because a root-treated tooth becomes brittle. Root canal treatment is always included in your written plan with the restoration shown separately — no hidden line items.

Comfort options

Three levels of comfort — you choose

Sedation is not reserved for surgery. If anxiety has kept you out of a dental chair, tell us and we will plan around it.

Level 1

Standard gentle care

Topical anaesthetic before every injection, fine-gauge needles, a stop signal agreed in advance and an unhurried slot. Included in all treatment fees.

  • ✓ No upcharge
  • ✓ Longer appointment on request
Level 2 Most requested

Nitrous oxide (relative analgesia)

You breathe a light mix of oxygen and nitrous oxide through a small nosepiece. Relaxation arrives within minutes and wears off completely before you leave the chair.

  • ✓ Fully awake and responsive
  • ✓ Can drive yourself home afterwards
Level 3

Intravenous sedation

A carefully titrated IV sedative for long surgical sessions or pronounced dental phobia, delivered with continuous monitoring and escort-home requirements.

  • ✓ Fitness assessment first
  • ✓ Chaperone required for discharge
