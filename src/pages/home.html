Why Dantola

Built for people who dread the dentist

Anxious patients are not a special case here — they are the design brief. Every visit starts with an explanation, a written plan and a pause before anything touches your teeth.

  • Written plan and price before treatment

    You approve a printed sequence and cost. Nothing is added mid-procedure without a conversation.

  • Pain-managed as standard

    Topical anaesthetic before every injection, fine-gauge needles and nitrous oxide available on request.

  • Digital diagnosis, not guesswork

    Intraoral scanning and low-dose digital x-rays show you exactly what we see, on screen, before you decide.

  • Follow-up that actually happens

    A WhatsApp check-in 48 hours after every surgical procedure, and a reminder when your next recall is due.

How a visit works

Four unhurried steps

The same sequence for a cleaning or a full-arch reconstruction — you always know what happens next and what it costs.

  1. 01

    Consult & listen

    A 30-minute first visit: your history, your concerns and a full soft-tissue and periodontal examination.

  2. 02

    Digital scan & imaging

    Intraoral scan plus low-dose x-rays or CBCT where clinically needed. Your images go home with you.

  3. 03

    Plan & price, in writing

    Options ranked by urgency and cost, with the evidence behind each recommendation and no pressure to decide today.

  4. 04

    Treat & follow up

    Comfort-managed treatment, a written aftercare sheet, and a WhatsApp check-in 48 hours later.

Clinical technology

Equipment that shortens treatment and lowers radiation

Technology only earns its place here if it improves your outcome or your comfort. These four do both — and each one is included in your diagnostic fee, not upsold on top of it.

See our standards
▣

3D CBCT imaging

Volumetric scans at roughly a tenth of the dose of a medical CT, used to plan implants and impacted teeth safely.

▤

Intraoral scanning

No impression trays and no gagging: a digital model in minutes, accurate enough for crowns, aligners and bridges.

☉

Digital smile design

Your own photographs are mapped to a proposed result, then trialled in your mouth before any enamel is prepared.

⚖

Guided implant surgery

Printed surgical guides that place implants to tenths of a millimetre, so healing is faster and swelling smaller.
