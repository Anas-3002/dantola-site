Our story

Grown slowly, on purpose

We have never opened a branch we could not staff with our own clinicians, and we do not measure success in chairs filled per hour. Appointments are scheduled with real time between them, because rushed dentistry is expensive dentistry — it gets redone.

  1. 2008

    Two chairs in DHA Phase 6, one dentist and a promise to explain every treatment before it starts.

  2. 2014

    First digital radiography suite in the practice; film x-rays retired and radiation dose cut sharply.

  3. 2019

    Dedicated implant and surgical suite opened, with guided surgery and a separate sterilisation room.

  4. 2024

    CBCT imaging and intraoral scanning brought in-house, so planning happens the same day as the scan.

“The most important piece of equipment in this clinic is the explanation. A patient who understands their own mouth makes better decisions than one who is simply told what to do.”

Dr. Hina Farooqi · Clinical Director

Standards & technology

What happens before you sit down

Sterilisation, water quality and material traceability are not visible to patients — which is exactly why we publish them.

Sterilisation

Class B vacuum autoclave

Instruments are cleaned in an ultrasonic bath, sealed in pouches and sterilised in a vacuum autoclave at every cycle, with a logged indicator in each pouch.

Water quality

Independent water lines

Each chair runs its own filtered line, flushed and tested on a fixed schedule — because dental unit water is a genuine infection-control issue.

Imaging

Low-dose digital radiology

Digital sensors and CBCT with collimated fields, so you receive the diagnostic information we need and no more radiation than that.

Materials

Traceable, CE-marked stock

Every implant, composite and ceramic carries a batch number recorded against your file, so any material can be traced years later.

What we hold to

Four commitments we will not trade away

Consent that means something

You are told the alternatives — including doing nothing — and the likely outcome of each. If you are not ready to decide, you leave with the plan and the time to think it over.

No treatment without a diagnosis

We do not sell packages before examination and imaging. Anything recommended to you is written down with the clinical reason beside it.

Your records are yours

Scans, x-rays, photographs and your treatment plan are shared with you on request, in a format you can hand to any other dentist.

Conservative first

Where a filling will do, we do not crown. Where a clean will do, we do not fill. Enamel removed is enamel gone for life.

First visit · 30 minutes

Come and meet us before you commit to anything

A consultation includes a full examination, digital imaging where needed and a written plan you can take home and think about.
